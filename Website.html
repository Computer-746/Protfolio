My Favourite Anime

The Fragrant Flower Blooms with Dignity

One of my favourite anime is The Fragrant Flower Blooms with Dignity. I enjoy the way the story shows friendship, school life, personal growth and relationships.

I like the combination of school life, friendship and romance. The characters learn to understand one another, and their relationships develop through small but meaningful moments.
