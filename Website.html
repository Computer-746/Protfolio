About Me

Hello! I’m Bhawana Basnet, a 16-year-old Class 11 Section G student currently studying at St. Xavier’s College. My birthday is on June 10, which falls on Jestha 27 in the Nepali calendar.

I consider myself a curious and creative person who enjoys learning new things and exploring different interests. I especially enjoy activities that allow me to be creative while also helping me learn something useful.

My personality often depends on my mood. When I am happy, I can be very extroverted and talkative, but when I am sad or upset, I usually become more quiet and introverted.

I sometimes struggle with anger, and I am trying to work on it and become better at managing my emotions. I also cry quite easily, whether I am sad, angry, or even laughing too hard.

I believe that these emotions are a part of me, and I am still learning more about myself. I want to keep improving and become a better and more confident version of myself.

My Favourite Anime

Sasaki to Miyano

One of my favourite anime is Sasaki to Miyano. I love how Sasaki listens to Miyano explaining the BL manga he reads and even includes himself in those conversations. I also love how the anime shows high school life through friendship, love, and small everyday moments.

The story makes high school life look warm, meaningful, and exciting. Sometimes while watching it, I find myself imagining a high school life filled with friendships and memories like the ones shown in the anime. The characters and their relationship are one of the main reasons I enjoy it so much.

Why I Like It

I like the natural way the characters interact with each other and how their relationship develops slowly. The friendship, humour, romance, and everyday school moments make the story special to me.

My Favourite Part

One of the things I enjoy most is when Sasaki listens to Miyano talk about the BL manga he reads and becomes interested in the conversation himself.

A Small Note

The anime makes ordinary school moments feel memorable, which is one reason I enjoy watching it.
